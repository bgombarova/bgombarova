#### **Horizon Health Marketing Analysis**
**Tools:** `Excel` • `Tableau`

Healthcare-focused analysis evaluating **multi-year marketing campaign performance** across **8.4M impressions and 16.3K signups** for a U.S. health insurance organization.

The project focused on **validating dataset completeness and consistency** before analysis to ensure accurate reporting across marketing and operational metrics.

Using Excel and Tableau, the analysis evaluated:

- Campaign performance trends  
- Customer acquisition efficiency  
- Downstream revenue impact  

The analysis highlighted **high-performing initiatives and inefficient campaigns with elevated cost-per-signup**, providing insights to support **marketing budget allocation and operational decision-making**.
