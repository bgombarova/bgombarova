Data analyst focused on revenue operations, pipeline performance, and operational analytics. Skilled in SQL, Excel, and Tableau with experience analyzing large real-world datasets to support reporting, forecasting visibility, and data-driven business decisions.

### Stakeholder Projects
-  **[Aventra Electronics E-Commerce Analysis](https://github.com/bgombarova/aventra-electronics-ecommerce-analysis)**  
  SQL • Excel • Tableau  
  Analysis of Aventra Electronics’ 108K+ e-commerce records on performance from 2019–2022, providing insight into how revenue, customer behavior, and product performance shifted around the pandemic. The analysis evaluates sales performance, refund trends, order volume, and loyalty program behavior using SQL, Excel, and Tableau.

  The project includes data validation, integrity checks, and anomaly detection to ensure reliable reporting before analysis. Results were translated into stakeholder dashboards that highlight key performance trends and operational insights.

The analysis identifies growth opportunities, operational inefficiencies, and revenue risks to support data-driven decisions across sales, marketing, and commercial operations.

-  **[Horizon Health Marketing Analysis](https://github.com/bgombarova/horizon-health-marketing-analysis)**  
  Excel • Tableau  
  Healthcare-focused analysis evaluating multi-year marketing campaign performance data covering 8.4M impressions and 16.3K signups for a U.S. health insurance organization.

  The project focuses on validating dataset completeness and consistency prior to analysis, ensuring reliable reporting across marketing and operational metrics. Using Excel and Tableau, campaign  performance, customer acquisition efficiency, and downstream revenue impact were evaluated.

  The analysis highlights high-performing initiatives and inefficient campaigns with elevated cost-per-signup, providing insight to support marketing budget allocation and operational decision-making.
